Synq

Your life, in one calm place.

Synq is a life app made in Nepal. It keeps your money, health, home, time and the people you care about in one simple place, with Nepali dates (Bikram Sambat) built in.

Your data stays on your phone. You can use Synq without an account. With an account you can back up and use Synq on another phone.

Money
Accounts, spending, budgets, bills and loans.
Health
Vitals, lab reports, plans and reminders.
Home
Shopping, food stock, gas cylinder and repairs.
Time & people
Calendar, events, birthdays and contacts.
