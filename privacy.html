Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Synq ("we") is a personal life app. This policy explains what data Synq uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have. In short: your data stays on your phone, we never sell it, and we never show ads.

1. Data kept on your phone

Everything you enter in Synq (money, health, notes, documents, contacts, events and so on) is stored on your own phone. You can use Synq fully without an account, and then nothing you enter leaves your phone.

2. Your account (optional)

If you create an account, we store your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the name and email Google shares with us. Accounts are managed by our backend provider, Supabase. We use this only to sign you in. We do not use Google data for anything else and never share it.

3. Backup and sync (only if you turn them on)

If you turn on backup or sync, a copy of your data is stored on our Supabase server so you can restore it or use Synq on another phone. Each person can only reach their own data. Backups can be locked with your own password.

4. Sharing (only if you turn it on)

You can choose to share some items with a partner or family group. Nothing is shared unless you turn it on, item by item.

5. AI features

Some optional features (like writing a wish) use Google Gemini through our server. Only the short text needed for that task is sent. Health, documents and money data are never sent to the AI.

6. Usage statistics and crash reports

To fix bugs and improve the app, Synq may send anonymous usage events (like which screen was opened) and crash reports through Google Firebase. These never include your health values, money amounts, notes, document details, names or any other personal content. There is no advertising ID and no ad tracking. You can turn this off in Settings.

7. Phone permissions

  • Bank SMS (optional): Synq reads payment messages from banks to suggest expenses. The messages are read on your phone and never leave it.
  • Health Connect (optional): steps, sleep and heart rate, shown only in the app on your phone.
  • Notifications, camera, files: used only for the feature you choose.

8. Feedback

If you send feedback, we receive your message (and screenshot, if you attach one) so we can reply and improve Synq.

9. Deleting your data

Data on your phone is removed when you delete it in the app or uninstall Synq. To delete your account and any backup or synced data on our server, email us at manojpokhrel1995@gmail.com from your account's email address. We delete it within 30 days.

10. Children

Synq is not meant for children under 13 without a parent's help.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

12. Contact

Questions? Email manojpokhrel1995@gmail.com.
